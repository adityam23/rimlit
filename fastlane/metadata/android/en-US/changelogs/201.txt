The first release on F-Droid. The app is the same as 0.2.0; only how it is built changed, so that F-Droid can reproduce it exactly.

New since 0.1:
- Looks: save up to ten named looks and give any app its own look or colors
- New edge styles: Wave, Glitter, Echo, Loop, Mirror, Boomerang and Fireworks
- Particles: eight built-in kinds, your own pictures, or the app's icon
- A pill with the app's icon and name, growing out of the camera cutout
- Export, import and share looks
