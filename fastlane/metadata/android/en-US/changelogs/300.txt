A redesigned Rimlit.

- Edit looks full screen, glowing round your real edge over your wallpaper, starting from a premade; changes are marked and can be reset
- Home shows your looks, the default first, with the apps using each
- New Apps screen: pick a look for each app, or switch apps off
- Guided first launch, with screen fit and the app pill's position
- Glow on only the sides you choose
- Type a color as a code, like #00BCD4
- Glows as short as 0.3 s
